okay so before this class i genuinely would have said the internet and the web are the same thing. turns out they're not. the internet is the infrastructure, like the actual cables and servers doing the heavy lifting underneath everything. the world wide web is what sits on top of that. it's the part we actually use every day: websites, browsers, links, urls. it's basically a space built inside the internet that anyone can walk into.

and that's the part that actually interests me. there's this artist maya man who has one of the most intentional websites i've ever come across. it doesn't feel like a portfolio, it feels like a whole world she built. and i think that's what a website can really be when someone puts thought into it. it's not just a page, it's an extension of a person. the fonts, the colors, the way things are laid out, whether things move or stay still, all of it is saying something. it's design as self expression.

that's why i think the web is so deeply connected to art. it gives artists a way to exist on their own terms without a gallery or an algorithm deciding who gets to see their work. just you and a url. i've found so many artists, musicians, and designers i love just from stumbling onto a website they made themselves. that kind of discovery feels real in a way that scrolling through instagram doesn't.

i came into this class with some html experience but honestly i never thought about it like this before. that shift feels important.
